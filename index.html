Blueberry Cheesecake

Introduction

Blueberry cheesecake is a rich and creamy dessert with a buttery biscuit base, smooth cheesecake filling, and a sweet blueberry topping.

It is perfect for birthdays, parties, or as a homemade treat.

Ingredients

For the Crust

  • 200 g digestive biscuits or graham crackers
  • 100 g melted butter

For the Cheesecake Filling

  • 500 g cream cheese
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup fresh cream
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour

For the Blueberry Topping

  • 1 cup blueberries
  • 3 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons water

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Prepare the Crust
    • Crush the biscuits into fine crumbs.
    • Mix the crumbs with melted butter.
    • Press the mixture into the bottom of a cake pan.
    • Refrigerate for 15 minutes.
  2. Step 2: Make the Filling
    • Beat the cream cheese until smooth.
    • Add sugar and mix well.
    • Add eggs one at a time.
    • Mix in vanilla extract, fresh cream, and flour.
    • Pour the filling over the crust.
  3. Step 3: Bake the Cheesecake
    • Preheat the oven to 160°C.
    • Bake for 45–50 minutes until the center is slightly set.
    • Let it cool completely.
    • Refrigerate for at least 4 hours.
  4. Step 4: Prepare the Blueberry Topping
    • Heat blueberries, sugar, and lemon juice in a pan.
    • Add the cornstarch mixture.
    • Stir until the sauce thickens.
    • Cool the topping and spread it over the cheesecake.

Serving Suggestion

Serve chilled with extra blueberries or whipped cream on top for a delicious dessert experience.
